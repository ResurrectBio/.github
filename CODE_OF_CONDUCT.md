# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Everyone interacting in Resurrect Bio repositories — issues, pull requests, discussions, or code reviews — is expected to help create a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or role.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Being respectful and considerate in communication, especially in writing where tone is easy to misread.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on the work, not the person.
- Assuming good intent, and asking clarifying questions before assuming the worst.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies within all Resurrect Bio repositories and any official communication channels tied to this org (issues, PRs, discussions, associated Google Teams channels, etc.).

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior can be reported to your line manager or senior leadership. Reports will be reviewed and addressed promptly and fairly. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.

Consequences for violations are decided case-by-case and may include a warning, temporary restriction from repos, or removal from the organisation, depending on severity.
